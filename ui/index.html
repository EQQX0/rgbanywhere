Calibrate colors

Every device now shows the test color. Adjust R, G or B on any device that looks off until they all match. 100% is no change. Start with white, then check your own color.

Diagnostics

Everything the app does is written to log files, including attempts that failed, with the technical details. That makes problems possible to track down and fix later.

Update RGB Anywhere

Setup downloads first (about 90 MB). Windows then asks for permission once. The app closes while Setup runs and opens again when it's done. Your colors, presets and profiles stay as they are. The lights may blink while the lighting service restarts.
